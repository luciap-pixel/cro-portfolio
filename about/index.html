Two developers who got tired of watching good businesses lose customers.

We are Jarek and Lucia. Both of us studied full stack development, and kept seeing the same thing: businesses that are genuinely good at what they do, with websites that hide it.

A great business with a weak website is losing money every day.

You can be the best builder, the best kitchen maker, the best brand in your market. If your website does not show it, the customer never finds out. They land, they doubt, they leave, and they buy from someone worse who simply looked more convincing online.

That is the part that bothers us. It is fixable. Most sites are not losing customers because the business is bad. They are losing them because the page was built to look finished, not to sell.

The work is split evenly and you deal with both of us directly. One designs the decision, the other builds it and measures it.

Let’s turn your traffic into revenue.
