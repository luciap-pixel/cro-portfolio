I. The Makers

A workshop, two founders,
and almost thirty years of
quiet craftsmanship.

Woodchester Cabinet Makers was founded in 1996 by Tim Buckingham and Lee Hathaway. Three decades on, every kitchen we make still passes through the same workshop, the same hands, the same installation team.

No showroom orders, no flat-pack carcasses, no subcontracted fitters. Traditional construction, solid timber, and joinery that will close softly thirty years from now.

II. How we work, your way

One workshop. Two ways
to work with us.

Every kitchen begins with a conversation. From there, you decide how involved you'd like us to be — we'll either handle the whole thing, or build just the cabinetry for your fitter.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Tell us a little about your project. We answer personally, usually within a working day.

  2. 02

    Design consultation

    Meet Tim or Lee at our Cotswolds workshop. We walk through your space, ideas and budget — no obligation.

  3. 03

    Hand-built in the Cotswolds

    Every cabinet is made by our own craftsmen using traditional construction.

  4. 04

    Installed by our team

    Our own fitters install the kitchen carefully — from first sketch to the final handle.
