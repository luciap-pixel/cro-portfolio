Frequently asked

Good questions, honest answers

Do you only work with British hardwood?

Almost exclusively, yes. Wherever possible we source our timber from British woodlands and mill it in our own workshop. It means we know exactly where each board came from — and we can keep our supply chain short and sustainable.

What types of wood do you stock?

English oak, ash, elm, walnut, sycamore and a regular rotation of more unusual finds — burr oak, burr elm, olive ash and the occasional fruitwood. Stock changes constantly, so it’s always worth asking what’s in the yard.

Is your timber kiln-dried or air-dried?

Both. Most of our slabs are kiln-dried on site to a stable furniture-grade moisture content. We also keep some air-dried boards for clients who prefer them — just ask.

Do you offer slab preparation?

Yes — flattening, thicknessing, sanding and finishing. If you’re a maker buying a slab from us, we can prep it to whatever stage you need.

Can I visit your workshop?

Absolutely, and we’d encourage it. Seeing slabs in person is the best way to choose one for a bespoke project. Drop us a message to arrange a time that suits.
