Questions

Before you subscribe

What exactly is in each box?

Every month you receive one full-size RUCI product, one full-size skincare or wellness product, and extra treats like face masks, ginger shots, face tools and body-care — all curated around a new monthly theme.

Why is there a 3-month minimum?

The 3-month minimum lets us source full-size products, plan each month's theme properly, and give you time to actually experience the rituals — most products show their best results after a few weeks of consistent use.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes — after your third box you can pause or cancel anytime from your account. No phone calls, no hoops.

When do boxes ship?

Billing is on the 1st of the month and boxes ship within the first week. Your very first box ships shortly after you subscribe.

Can I give it as a gift?

Absolutely. Every box arrives wrapped with a bow, so it looks beautiful straight out of the parcel — perfect to gift unopened.
