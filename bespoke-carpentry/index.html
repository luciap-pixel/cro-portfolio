Choose your timber

Seven hardwoods

Each timber has its own tone, grain, and character. Because every board is unique, the piece you receive will never be an exact match to any photo — that's the point.

Tulipwood

Pale, even grain. The most affordable option.

Sapele

Deep reddish-brown with a lustrous figure.

Beech

Light, warm and consistent. Fine texture.

Ash

Bold, open grain in a bright pale-cream tone.

Iroko

Rich golden-brown, dense and hard-wearing.

Oak

The classic — warm honey tone, distinctive grain.

Walnut

Deep chocolate tones with striking figuring.
