About

Two developers who got tired of watching good businesses lose customers.

We are Jarek and Lucia. We both studied full stack development, and we kept seeing the same thing: businesses that are genuinely good at what they do, with websites that hide it. So we built Konar Studio to fix exactly that.

Why we do this

A great business with a weak website is losing money every single day.

You can be the best builder, the best kitchen maker, the best brand in your market. If your website does not show it, the customer never finds out. They land, they doubt, they leave, and they buy from someone worse who simply looked more convincing online.

That is the part that bothers us. It is fixable. Most sites are not losing customers because the business is bad. They are losing them because the page was built to look finished, not to sell.

So that is our whole job. We find where your site loses people, rebuild those moments around a clear decision, and we stay on it until the numbers move. We do not hand over a nice looking site and disappear.

Why us

What makes us different.

Plenty of people will build you a website. Here is what you get with us that you do not get from most.

We chase the number, not applause

Most studios are done when the site looks good. That is where we start. We care about enquiries, sales and sign ups going up, and we set up the tracking to prove whether they did. A beautiful site that does not sell is a failure to us.

Design and code under one roof

We are both full stack developers, so the person designing your page and the person building it actually understand each other. Nothing gets designed that cannot be built well, and nothing gets built that quietly drifts from the plan.

We stay until it works

Launch day is the start, not the finish. We keep improving the page based on what the data shows, so the site gets better after it goes live instead of slowly ageing. You are not left on your own with something you cannot change.

Straight answers, no inflated claims

We will tell you honestly when something you have asked for will hurt the site, and we will never promise a number we cannot control. The work you see here is labelled for exactly what it is. That is the studio we would want to hire.
