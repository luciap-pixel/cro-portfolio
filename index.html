FAQ

Straight answers.

  • These are concept redesigns — have you done this for real clients?

    These are self-initiated redesigns I built to show how I think and work. Every decision is grounded in real conversion principles — I’d apply the same process to your live page and your data.

  • How much does it cost?

    It depends on scope — a single landing page is very different from a full funnel. Start with the free teardown; if we move forward, you get a fixed price before any work begins.

  • I already have a designer or developer.

    Perfect — I can work alongside them. My focus is conversion: what to change and why. They can build it, or I can.

  • What do I actually get?

    A redesigned page in Shopify or Webflow, plus a plain-English breakdown of every change and the reasoning behind it — so you’re never guessing.

  • Will this definitely raise my conversion rate?

    No one honest can promise a number. What I promise is a page built on sound conversion principles with a clear rationale for every decision — so you’re making informed bets, not guesses.
